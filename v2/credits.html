Credits

The 3D map is made from open data. Each source is listed with the licence it is used under.

OpenStreetMap

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, data available under the Open Database License.

Used for: roads, walking paths, building outlines outside campus, street lamps, some trees, the lakes and creeks (the water layer). The data files this map downloads that are made from OpenStreetMap are themselves offered under the Open Database License (ODbL 1.0); each one says so in its own header and is kept separate from the City's data.

City of Austin

Aerial imagery (January 2025) and street data: City of Austin, Texas. Data retrieved from Data.AustinTexas.gov (https://data.austintexas.gov) and the City of Austin GIS Data (https://geohub.austintexas.gov). Not endorsed by the City of Austin.

Used for: the ground photograph (the City's 2025 6-inch aerial), sidewalks, curb ramps, crosswalks and stop lines, traffic signals, and the trees (the City's tree inventories, and where they have none, crowns placed on the canopy the City's aerial shows). The City offers this data "free and without restriction" under its Open Data Terms of Use and asks for credit; it "cannot vouch for their quality and timeliness". The near ground tiles are loaded from the City's own map server.

Texas Geographic Information Office

Terrain and 2021 aerial fallback: Texas Geographic Information Office (TxGIO) StratMap, CC0.

Used for: building heights from the 2021 Travis County lidar (public domain, CC0 1.0).

The University of Texas at Austin

Campus building data © The University of Texas at Austin.
