# Stable coverage contract — round 2

The candidate remains an opt-in experiment until the quality, cost and construction gate all pass. The round-2 quality bar is the owner’s published round-1 lattice result per view, not the superseded 0.16 threshold. Finite 8-bit output has a measured flicker floor; that fact does not excuse cost or missing future-content coverage.

The renderer samples visibility on a 4×4 spatial grid while preserving native material gradients. Explicit texture gradients remove the West Campus facade-filter mismatch. Exact geometry packing/welding preserves every indexed attribute tuple, and releases superseded buffers and staging arrays. Hardware comparison filtering and conservative bounds reduce shadow reads; bounds reuse the existing shadow colour attachments. The optional daylight variant removes legacy lighting only when sunlight’s blend weight is exactly one and night lighting exactly zero; twilight and night use original materials. Fully faded surface terms may be skipped. All sixteen samples belong to the current frame; the delivered spatial path uses no temporal history.

`?samplelattice=1&samplegrad=1&samplelight=1&samplepcf=1&samplebounds=1&samplework=1&samplecompact=1&sampleday=1` loads the integrated candidate. `MoireRuntime.ready` resolves after initial compaction and activation. `sampletiles=4` trades more drawing passes for one quarter of temporary target storage. Tiling, depth prefill, history and lower sample counts are separately measurable experiments; their existence is not evidence that their costs or quality pass. Compaction adds startup CPU work and does not automatically compact later geometry. Remove the query switches and reload for the original rendering path. Live-off releases the sample target but retains compacted geometry and compiled shader hooks.

The app’s delivered script omits comments and whitespace only. Readable source is `src/app.js`; reproduce it with `scripts/build-moire-app.mjs` and the pinned installation command in that file. No minifier compression or name mangling is enabled. This source-size saving must be included when comparing actual downloaded bytes.

Acceptance runs `scripts/verify/moire-r2-gate.mjs`. It requires all six views at rest, consecutive one-pixel steps and actual pointer-drag replay against fixed original-main 16-jitter references. Published two-decimal thresholds receive a stated 0.005 rounding interval; West step error/flicker must additionally be at most 0.30/0.40. Three cold interleaved main/candidate runs on Intel, AMD laptop and the defined phone profile must show no increase in p50, p90, allocated WebGL storage, page V8 heap plus backing storage, or worker-inclusive encoded body bytes. Source/evidence hashes prevent stale results passing. Emulation does not prove physical-phone behavior; page V8 and WebGL allocations do not include process/driver memory.

**Future content is not yet guaranteed by construction.** Sixteen point samples can alias arbitrary new geometry or shaders. A certified generator must replace repeated subpixel features with a pixel-integrated representation that preserves material fractions, lighting response, depth, visibility and silhouettes. All bake and shader entry points must participate; unsupported classes must fail generation. That registry, visibility-preserving proxy representation and complete proof are not implemented here. The concrete next rule and limits are in `out/future-content-rule.md`. The gate fails absent construction proof; its synthetic self-test is only a test of the checker.
