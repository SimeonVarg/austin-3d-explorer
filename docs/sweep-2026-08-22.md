The first sweep that finished. 38 frames: 12 poses x day (p=0.14) / dusk (p=0.5)
/ night (p=0.92), plus both reel-shot URL modes. Every frame below was opened
and read by eye, not diffed and skimmed. All frames in `shots/f/sweep/`; the

## Findings, ranked by what a visitor notices first

### 1. A day-pale patch of far terrain glows on the night horizon at the SPAWN hero pose

`sw-H1-spawn-night.png` — looking WSW from spawn at p=0.92, a broad pale
pink-white sheet of far ground sits right on the horizon, bright against an
otherwise black city, with more faint pale strips beside it. It reads as a
chunk of daytime terrain the night grade never touched, at the one pose every
visitor who presses nothing ever sees. This is exactly the class
`night-pale.mjs` was recalibrated to police (K4's own text: the result was
never looked at) — whoever owns that script should point it at this pose;
I could not establish which layer the patch is (far basemap, outer ring, or
water) from frames alone.

### 2. The far ring draws bright mint-green vegetation outlines that ignore the time of day

Worst at night: `sw-H4-city-night.png` (top third) shows glowing green
squiggly polygon outlines strewn across the dark far field — they read as
green wireframe static on the horizon. Same green marks at dusk
(`sw-H4-city-dusk.png`, green speckles on the orange far field) and, muted,
at day (`sw-H4-city-day.png`, `sw-downtown-day.png`). Every wide shot at any
hour shows some of it; night is the loud case. Looks like far-ring
landuse/greenbelt geometry keeping its daylight green while everything around
it is graded — `js/outer.js` territory, which this lane may not touch, so:
report only, frames above are the evidence.

### 3. Lens-flare ghost rings paint on buildings when the sun itself is off-screen

`sw-westcampus-dusk.png` — two large translucent rings sit ON the facade of
the foreground tower like soap bubbles; the sun is not in frame.
`sw-H3-tower-dusk.png` and `sw-H5-dkr-dusk.png` have fainter mid-frame rings;
`sw-H1-spawn-dusk.png` has a stray yellow ghost blob in the top-right corner.
Day case: `sw-westcampus-day.png` shows a rust-red vertical streak smeared
across the same tower's flank. A flare chain that only makes sense pointing
at a visible sun is being drawn regardless. (Frames only — I did not isolate
which FX layer; no code was read for this.)

### 4. Downtown still reads cooler and greyer than campus in every wide day frame

`sw-H2-dragcorr-day.png`, `sw-downtown-day.png`, `sw-H5-dkr-day.png` — the
known K5 (QUEUE, re-measured 2026-08-22, honest refusal on record: no
one-file fix exists). Confirmed still true, not new, listed so the ranking is
honest: it is the most visible taste gap in the daytime city after the two
defects above.

### 5. Labels: giant near-camera names, and names that shine through buildings

Near-camera labels render enormous (`sw-H3-tower-day.png` bottom: "Robert A.
Welch Hall" at ~40px; `sw-southmall-day.png` bottom right: "University
Christian Church"). And in `sw-southmall-day.png` the labels of five halls
BEHIND Welch Hall draw on top of its facade. Both are engine label behaviour
visible in the committed C-HERO set too (C-HERO2's cropped giant "24"), i.e.
pre-existing, not from this week — but a first-time viewer does notice.
