## Nov 10 2026 — photo skin: walls keep the pixels of their photographs (#665, `mac/v2-photo-skin`)

- **What:** `scripts/v2/photo_skin.mjs` (bake on the Mac, about a minute, no model call; apply in the tile build). A building's
  faces are cut into flat rectangles; each texel takes its colour from the photograph that sees it best (depth test, tree/sky/car
  masks, one trusted photograph per rectangle, tone-matched fill for what no photograph saw, small relief drawn with its wall).
  Each wall is kept as sharp as its photograph was there. The tile holds the atlas at half size; `v2/tiles.js` loads the full one
  (`v2/skins/<slug>/albedo.jpg`) for the two nearest skinned buildings on a desktop.
- **First building:** Hogg Memorial Auditorium, front and south-east corner (tile `t_-1_-1`; the other 7 buildings identical).
  The north side keeps its build: oaks hide it in the photographs.
- **Checked:** live on the Pages mirror and the host after the merge (tile and atlas equal `main`); same camera as the photographs (closer than live: real door glass and lobby, joints, medallions, stains); five
  lights (default, morning sun, afternoon, sunset, night): the skin takes the app's light; a square-on view no photograph was
  taken from and a 66 degree arc. Shimmer meter main / branch: 120 m -0.09 / -0.09, 600 m and north equal, east 250 m
  -0.07 / -0.01 (the ship gate marks that line red against main; both are at or below zero, reason in the pull request).
