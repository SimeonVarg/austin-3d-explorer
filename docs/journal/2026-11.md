## Nov 10 2026 — photo skins for Rowling Hall and the AT&T Center entrance (#666, `mac/v2-skin-batch1`)

- **Live** (tile `t_-1_1` and the atlas on the Pages mirror and the host equal `main`; the live site looked at from the photograph's camera). Rowling: the court side (fins, glass front, lit ground floor, entrance). AT&T: the stone entrance bay, window, lanterns, steps.
- **Bake changes:** the colour factor is the sum of the build's wall colours over the sum of what the photographs show (a mean of ratios gave Rowling 0.91 where 0.42 is right); the build's own parts (signs, lamps, rails) are masked in each photograph, so a sign is not drawn twice; a bake may set its own texture bias (Rowling 0).
- **Shimmer meter, main / branch:** Rowling east 250 m 0.15 / 0.11, east 600 m 0.58 / 0.44, south-east 250 m 0.11 / 0.13; AT&T equal on all four views.

- **What the method is good for:** parts with detail that does not repeat (entrances, carved stone, glass with rooms behind it, cornices), from a close daytime photograph with no lamps on.

## Nov 10 2026 — photo skin: walls keep the pixels of their photographs (#665, `mac/v2-photo-skin`)

- **What:** `scripts/v2/photo_skin.mjs` (bake on the Mac, about a minute, no model call; apply in the tile build). A building's
  faces are cut into flat rectangles; each texel takes its colour from the photograph that sees it best (depth test, tree/sky/car
  masks, one trusted photograph per rectangle, tone-matched fill for what no photograph saw, small relief drawn with its wall).
  Each wall is kept as sharp as its photograph was there. The tile holds the atlas at half size; `v2/tiles.js` loads the full one
  (`v2/skins/<slug>/albedo.jpg`) for the two nearest skinned buildings on a desktop.
