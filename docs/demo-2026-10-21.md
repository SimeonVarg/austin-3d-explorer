## SAFE DEMO PATH

Written from the pictures above. The camera presets were set by script, not flown with real keys from the home pose; fly times use the measured speeds (W 36 m/s, Shift+W 91 m/s). Rehearse the keys once on the Acer.

0. **Network first.** On the demo machine's network, open /v2/ and check it gets to the flight in under about 30 s. On 10/21 at 1 am this Mac's route to Vercel crawled (5 to 25 KB/s) and the city never loaded; if that happens, switch network (phone hotspot) or use v1.
1. Open `https://flyover-utx.vercel.app/v2/` about two minutes before the call and let it sit (8 to 14 s load here on a good network; v1 is the fallback). Do not touch anything during the opening flight (about 13 s after the lift): it starts over downtown and ends over campus, the best-looking minute of the app.
2. Click "Where should I live?", type "Computer", Enter. Click **Jester West Hall** (#1, the camera flies to it) and say: new building built from photos. Stay at this height; `finder5-1-jester-card.png` is the picture to expect. Then click **Dobie Twenty21** and **Walks to class** (`finder-5-walk.png`).
3. To show the bus: pick a West Campus home with "walk + bus" in its row (The Block, #43) and tick "Show live buses" (`finder5-3-bus.png`). The camera zooms far out; after that close the finder (x) and press **R** to come home.
4. Close the finder. With the Tower in the middle of the screen, hold **W** (Shift for faster) toward the Tower and stop about 300 m away and 150 m up, looking down: that is `demo-checks/tower-far.png`, the best frame. Pan with drag; names stay on their buildings.
5. From the same height, look at the new campus buildings from above: **Batts/Mezes/Benedict** (`views/batts-far.png`), **Calhoun Hall** (`v2-light/calhoun-aerial-sw.small.jpg`, SW aerial), **Jester West Hall** (`views/jester-aerial-s.png`). From above they read as real buildings; do not drop to eye level on them.
6. Turn east (drag) and fly about 600 m to DKR; stop high (about 200 m up, `demo-checks/dkr-far.png`; the 10/20 SW aerial `v2-light/LIVE-dkr-aerial-sw.jpg` is the same idea). A slow drag around the bowl from there is fine; `demo-checks/dkr-near.png` is also acceptable now.
7. Street level, one place only: **Congress at 6th** (`street/downtown-street.png`, 8 m up and 45 m back, the cleanest street frame; `street/congress-eye.png` at 1.8 m is also good). If flying there is awkward, the Drag at Guadalupe/21st at 8 m is the second choice (`street/drag-street.png`). Do not wander; no other junction was checked tonight.
8. Finish with **R**, which eases home. If anything misbehaves, open `https://flyover-utx.vercel.app/` (v1; it loaded, slowly, 28 to 35 s lift on 10/20).
